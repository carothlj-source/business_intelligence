# Airbnb Analysis
Lindsey Carothers

---

1. Hosts with ten or more listings hold 37.15% of Columbus’s listings, compared with 31.90% in Chicago and 17.16% in the Twin Cities, showing that large hosts have the strongest presence in Columbus.

2. The highest-earning 10% of Columbus listings generate 36.08% of the city’s estimated revenue, meaning a small portion of listings accounts for over one-third of estimated earnings.

3. Only 19.0% of Columbus entire-home listings are mostly taken for October 2026, compared with 20.0% in the Twin Cities and 21.8% in Chicago, giving Columbus the lowest share of mostly-taken homes.

4. The Twin Cities received 7,399 reviews in August 2025, making it the busiest month for reviews during the July 2025 through June 2026 period.

5. Superhosts charge a median $43.80 more in Chicago and $40.50 more in Columbus, while Twin Cities Superhosts charge $1.38 less than other hosts, showing that the Superhost price premium varies considerably by city.
